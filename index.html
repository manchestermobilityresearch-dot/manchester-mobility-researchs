Why we're asking

Before developing anything, we want to understand the people who provide and use ride-hailing services every day. Participation is voluntary. We do not need payment details, licence numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information. If you would like to take part in a future research interview, you can optionally leave a contact email in the driver survey.
